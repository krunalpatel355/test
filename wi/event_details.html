About This Event

Get ready for the ultimate summer experience! The Summer Music Festival 2025 brings together the hottest artists, incredible food, and an atmosphere you'll never forget. Join thousands of music lovers for three days of non-stop entertainment.

Featuring headliners like Taylor Swift, The Weeknd, and Billie Eilish, plus over 50 supporting acts across 4 stages. From indie rock to electronic dance music, there's something for every musical taste.

What's Included:
  • Access to all performances and stages
  • Food and beverage vendors
  • Art installations and interactive experiences
  • Free WiFi throughout the venue
  • On-site medical and security services
